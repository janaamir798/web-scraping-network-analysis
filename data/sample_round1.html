Season Description
The 2023–24 Premier League was the 32nd season of the Premier League and the 125th season of top-flight English football overall. The season began on 11 August 2023 and concluded on 19 May 2024. Manchester City, the defending champions, won their fourth consecutive title, the first team to do so in English men's football.
